# Architecture

## Overview
Twelve Ten Gallery website: a Vue 3 single-page app built with Vite and styled with Tailwind CSS 4, plus a TypeScript API (Hono) backed by Postgres via Kysely. All content (exhibitions, fairs, slideshows, news, press, home splash) lives in the database and is read by the site through `/api`. An admin-only back office at `/admin` manages people, artist and collector profiles, the works inventory (private prices and sales), invoices, private online viewing rooms (gated links to a selection of works, with per-visitor engagement tracking) and inquiries from the public site. Every uploaded image gets smaller copies for display and a high-resolution copy for zooming.

## Frontend (`apps/web`)
- Entry: `src/main.js` mounts `App.vue` with `vue-router` (`src/router.ts`, HTML5 history, every page lazy-loaded), `@unhead/vue` (document head), and a capture-phase `error` listener that swaps a rich-text image with a missing derivative back to its `data-original`.
- Routes: `/`, `/about`, `/exhibitions`, `/exhibitions/:slug`, `/fairs`, `/fairs/:slug`, `/news`, `/news/:slug`, `/documents/beech-von-sternberg-interview`, `/admin/login` (signing in goes to `next` or `/admin`), the back office under `/admin` (below), private viewing rooms at `/rooms/:token` (below), legacy redirects from `REDIRECTS`, and a catch-all 404.
- `App.vue` renders routes with `meta.backoffice` or `meta.bare` (viewing rooms) on their own (no site header, footer or editing bar).
- Data: `src/api/client.ts` (`api.*` typed fetchers against same-origin `/api`). `useResource` loads data reactively with loading / not-found / error state. `useMeta` sets title, description, canonical, Open Graph / Twitter tags and JSON-LD from `buildMeta`.
- Exhibitions and fairs: `pages/ExhibitionDetail.vue` loads one by slug and renders `components/exhibition/ExhibitionTemplate.vue`: optional slideshow (`slideshowVisible`), hero image (two columns) or a single narrow column when there is no hero, `ExhibitionHeader.vue` (artists/title/subtitle, PDF and header links, derived dates and events, venue, hours, Inquire button), and the body (`RichContent.vue` renders the API's `contentHtml` and replaces the `pressList` placeholder with the exhibition's press).
- Lists: `pages/Exhibitions.vue` (thumbnail grid) and `pages/Fairs.vue` (text cards) group items into Current / Upcoming / Past with `exhibitionStatus` on the gallery's local date.
- News: `pages/News.vue` shows Press and Gallery News side by side; each list loads 10 items at a time as the reader scrolls (`useInfiniteList`: IntersectionObserver sentinel with a 600px margin, no concurrent fetches, retry on error, a keyboard-reachable "Load more", `aria-live` announcements). Loaded items and scroll position persist for the session so back navigation restores them. `/news/:slug` shows one post.
- Images (`components/ArtImage.vue`): images under `/media` start from their derivatives (`srcset` of WebP widths with a `sizes` hint per use, `loading="lazy"` unless `eager`), with the tiny `w48` copy as a background until they load. Once loaded, if the copy is drawn wider than it has real pixels (high-density screens, `object-fit: cover`, a box larger than `sizes`), the next larger copy is fetched in the background and swapped in silently, keeping the same layout size (a one-entry `srcset` with its real width), up to the zoom copy and then the original; missing copies are skipped. `sharpest` (the home splash, the exhibition hero, the slide on show, the viewing room work) always continues up to the zoom copy. It checks again when its box grows. If the first pick is missing, the image shows the original. Used for slideshow, hero, exhibition list, home, viewing room and back-office images. Rich-text figures get the same `srcset` from the renderer.
- Zoom (`components/ZoomViewer.vue`): a full-screen inspector teleported to `body`. It opens on the large display copy (`w2400`) and loads the zoom copy (`zoom.jpg`, up to 6000 px) in the background, swapping it in silently without moving the view (the original if either is missing). Wheel and pinch zoom around the pointer, drag to pan, double-click / double-tap toggles 3×, buttons for − / + / fit / close, keyboard (`+ - 0`, arrows pan when zoomed or move between images, `Esc`), focus kept inside and returned on close, page scroll locked. Zoom stops at two screen pixels per image pixel (at least 2×). Emits the zoom level after each gesture and pointer positions over the image (0–1, at most four a second) with the zoom level. The exhibition slideshow (`GallerySlideshow.vue`) opens it from a magnifier in the toolbar (image slides only) and from scroll-view images, and returns to the last image viewed.
- Scroll View (`GallerySlideshow.vue`): a full-screen list of every slide; each image has its own Zoom button (clicking the picture does nothing); it closes with the round close button (top right, always above the content, focused on open) or Escape (Escape closes the zoom viewer first when it is open).
- Viewing rooms (`pages/ViewingRoom.vue`, `components/rooms/`): their own header ("Twelve Ten", "Private viewing room") and footer, `noindex`. The page asks `/api/rooms/:token` for the room; without the cookie `RoomGate` (title and subtitle) asks for name and email (with the tracking notice) and shows the room on success. `?preview=1` shows admins the room with a banner and no tracking. `RoomViewer`: title, optional subtitle, welcome and "Download PDF" (once an admin has generated one and offers it; in admin preview it uses `?preview=1`), optional intro; Works and Artist(s) tabs; slideshow (prev/next halves, arrow keys, thumbnails for more views, caption with price or status, Zoom, Inquire, About the artist (that artist only, with "Back to the work" and "See all artists"), counter) or grid (captioned tiles that open the slideshow), the choice remembered in `localStorage`; Inquire (available works only) opens the inquiry form in room mode. Zoom covers every image of the works on show, with the caption and Inquire. `RoomArtists`: name, portrait, bio, CV behind "Read CV" with a PDF link, press list, and "View their works in this room" (filters the works).
- Inquiries (`components/InquiryDialog.vue`, `composables/useVisitor.ts`): every Inquire button (the exhibition header when `showInquire`, works-available slides in `GallerySlideshow`, viewing rooms) opens a native `<dialog>` showing the item (image, caption, price when shown). A known visitor (`/api/visitor`; inside a room, the room's viewer) sees "Inquiring as …" ("Not you?" outside rooms forgets the browser) and only writes a note; others give name and email too. The note is plain text, up to 1000 characters with a counter; a hidden honeypot field catches bots. After sending: "Thank you — we'll be in touch." In admin preview of a room nothing is sent.
- Tracking (`composables/useRoomTracker.ts`): `v-track-work` / `v-track-artist` directives observe elements (IntersectionObserver, at least half visible). Every second while the tab is visible and there was input in the last minute, active time is added, plus view time for works in view, hover time for the work under the pointer, reading time for artists in view, and zoom time for the work in the zoom viewer (which then counts alone). Pointer samples over work images (at most four a second, up to 4000 per batch) and from the zoom viewer, focus / click / zoom / view-mode / artist events and counts (inquiries are recorded by the API) are batched and sent every 10 s and with `sendBeacon` on `pagehide` or when the tab is hidden; a batch that fails with a network or 5xx error is merged back for the next send. Nothing is sent without a `visitId`.
- Home: `pages/Home.vue` renders the splash from `/api/home` (desktop/mobile background with focal point, headline, subline, extra text, derived dates, black/white text) and links it to the chosen exhibition or fair.
- Styling: `src/style.css` imports Tailwind, adds `packages/shared/src` as a class source (classes emitted by the rich-text renderer), and defines theme fonts. SFC `<style>` blocks that use `@apply` start with `@reference "@/style.css";`. Global element styles in `App.vue` live in `@layer base` so utility classes override them.
- Dev server and `vite preview` proxy `/api` to `API_TARGET` (default `http://localhost:3000`, with `X-Forwarded-For`) and `/media/*` to the media bucket on LocalStack (`MEDIA_TARGET`, `MEDIA_BUCKET`).

## In-place editing (`apps/web/src/admin`)
- Session: `src/session.ts` checks `/api/session` only when this browser has signed in before (a `localStorage` hint set by `/admin/login`). `App.vue` mounts `admin/AdminLayer.vue` (a separate chunk) only when the user is an admin; the editors (`ExhibitionEditor`, `HomeEditor`, `EditableNewsEntry`, `EditablePressEntry`) and Tiptap are further lazy chunks, so public visitors download none of it.
- Edit mode (`admin/state.ts`): `editMode.editing` (kept in `sessionStorage` across reloads), the current page's `PageEditor` (dirty flag, save, discard, publish, history, settings, delete), toasts, and a conflict prompt. Pages register with `useEditablePage`; `useUnsavedGuard` and a router guard warn before leaving with unsaved changes.
- Admin bar (`AdminLayer.vue`): shown while editing — an "Admin" link to the back office at the far left, page label, Draft badge, unsaved indicator, Published switch, Discard, a create menu (exhibition, fair, news post, press item), a cog menu (page settings, History to restore any revision, delete the page, recently deleted, re-publish all pages, stop editing, sign out), with Save (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`) centred between the page details (left) and the controls (right). A floating "Edit site" button (Line Awesome `la-tools` icon, left edge) turns editing on.
- Exhibitions and fairs (`ExhibitionEditor.vue`): the same layout and classes as the public template, with artists/title (`InlineText`, plain-text `contenteditable`), dates (`DateRangePicker`, Reka UI range calendar), events (`EventsEditor`: label, day, start/end time in the exhibition's time zone), venue and hours inline; hero image and press-release PDF upload/replace/remove; body in `RichEditor` (Tiptap with the shared schema: paragraph styles, bold/italic/link, lists, image with caption and alt text, YouTube video, bio, press list, undo/redo). Page fields are saved together with a version check; a 409 opens the conflict prompt (reload theirs / overwrite). `ExhibitionSettings` covers type, web address, subtitle, list location, time zone, title-first, Inquire button, header links, thumbnail, hero alt text and SEO overrides with a live share-card preview.
- Slideshow (`SlideshowEditor.vue`): shown above the page while editing — show/hide slideshow, works available, drag to reorder (SortableJS), multi-file upload of images and MP4s, per-slide caption/alt editing, replace file, hide, mark available, delete. Slide changes save immediately.
- Home (`HomeEditor.vue`): desktop/mobile background upload, focus point picked by clicking the image, text colour, headline/subline/extra line inline, link target (any exhibition or fair), optional dates when unlinked, SEO overrides.
- News page: each post and press item becomes editable in place and is saved on its own (title, date picker, body in `RichEditor` news mode; outlet, title, date or issue label, byline, link, linked exhibition), with publish toggles, delete and history; "+ New post" / "+ Add press" create drafts at the top.
- Uploads (`admin/upload.ts`): request a presigned URL from `/api/admin/uploads`, then `PUT` the file directly to S3 with progress; for images, then tell `/api/admin/uploads/complete` (not waited for).
- UI primitives (`admin/ui/`): Reka UI dialog, switch, dropdown, popover and calendars styled with Tailwind; admin styles in `admin/admin.css` (loaded with the admin chunk).
- Slide links (`SlideshowEditor`, `LinkSlideDialog`): each slide tile shows its inventory link as an artist chip and a work chip (opening `/admin/people/:id/artist` and `/admin/works/:id` in a new tab), or a "Link artist" button; an "Unlinked images: N" badge sits above the grid. Linking looks the artist up with the back office's `PersonPicker` (search starts from the slide's artist caption; the exhibition's linked artists rank first; "Create new person" is offered), then either creates a work from the slide's caption and image or picks one of the artist's existing works. The caption dialog can change or remove the link.
- `@` resolves to `apps/web/src`.

## Back office (`apps/web/src/backoffice`)
- Admin-only pages under `/admin`, a separate lazy chunk with its own layout (`BackofficeLayout.vue`: the gallery name in Germania One, a sidebar with People, Artists, Collectors, Inventory, Invoices, Inquiries (with the number not yet dismissed), Viewing rooms and Duplicates (with the number of suggested pairs); both counts refresh on each page change), the new-inquiry alert above every page, a drawer on phones, "View site", sign-out, toasts and the conflict prompt, `noindex`). The route's `beforeEnter` loads the session and sends non-admins to `/admin/login?next=…`. Reuses the editor's Reka UI primitives (`admin/ui/`: `Dialog`, `Field`, `SelectField`, …), `RichEditor`, `HistoryDialog`, `MediaButton` and `upload.ts`.
- Routes: `/admin` → `/admin/people`; `/admin/people` (`PeopleList`); `/admin/people/:id/:profile?` (`PersonPage`); `/admin/works/:id` (`WorkDetail`; `new` with `?artist=` for a new work); `/admin/inventory` (`InventoryList`); `/admin/invoices` (`InvoicesList`); `/admin/invoices/new` (`InvoiceNew`); `/admin/invoices/settings` (`InvoiceSettings`); `/admin/invoices/:id` (`InvoicePage`); `/admin/people/duplicates` (`DuplicatesList`); `/admin/inquiries` (`inquiries/InquiriesList`); `/admin/rooms` (`rooms/RoomsList`); `/admin/rooms/:id` (`rooms/RoomEditor`; `new` for a new room); `/admin/rooms/:id/dashboard` (`rooms/RoomDashboard`); `/admin/rooms/:id/visitors/:visitorId` (`rooms/VisitorDashboard`).
- People list: one table of everyone with a profile-type filter (All, one entry per `PROFILE_TYPES` type, No profile), search (name, email, phone, city), sort (sort name, recently updated) and page, all kept in the URL; changing a filter returns to page 1. Columns: name, email, phone, city, and profiles (with the Artists filter: works, available works, last exhibition instead; with the Collectors filter: works collected, artists collected, open invoices). "New person" can add artist and collector profiles. Numbered pages of `PEOPLE_PAGE_SIZE` (25) with "Showing 1–25 of N", Previous/Next and page numbers (`Pagination.vue`). Rows are links (the name link covers the row); with a profile filter the row opens that profile's tab.
- Person page: details first (portrait, name, sort name, date and place of birth, website, private notes, email, phone, other emails (aliases; added and removed immediately); one Save with version check, Merge…, History, Delete when the person has no profiles), a note listing up to three possible duplicates (reasons, "Not the same", "Compare & merge"), addresses (`AddressesEditor`: structured address cards with label, primary, reorder, edit, remove; saved immediately; reusable for any `AddressEntity`), purchases (works this person bought; hidden when they have a collector profile, which shows them by artist), then the profile tabs. The active tab is the `:profile` URL segment; "+ Add profile" offers missing types. Panels are registered per type in `profiles/index.ts`.
- Artist panel (`profiles/ArtistProfilePanel.vue`): inventory prefix (editable, with the next number), bio and CV in `RichEditor` (news mode), "Download PDF" for the CV, linked exhibitions (add/remove, linking to the public pages), press (newest first: every press item about a linked exhibition plus press linked to the artist directly; outlet, date or issue label, title linking to the article or uploaded file, byline, the exhibition or "External press", "Not on site" when unpublished; "+ Add press" opens a dialog for outside coverage with a link or an uploaded PDF/image, and directly linked items can be edited or removed; exhibition press is edited on the News page), works gallery (cover image with title, date, medium, dimensions, inventory number, status and price; status filter; "+ Add work"), History and Remove profile.
- Collector panel (`profiles/CollectorProfilePanel.vue`): their inquiries (each with an anchor `#inquiry-<id>`, the item linking to the work, exhibition page or room, the note, when and where, what they typed and how they were matched, New / dismissed, Dismiss and Delete; reloads when an alert is dismissed), the artists they collect (chips linking to each artist's profile, with counts), then the collection grouped by artist (A–Z, each heading linking to the artist) as work cards; their invoices (number, date, works, total, balance, status); viewing rooms they entered (room linking to the visitor dashboard, "Inquired", what they typed at the gate when it differs, last visit, visits, active time, works seen, engagement dots) and the works they looked at most (top 6 with an interest bar); "+ New invoice" (choose among the works on hold for them, optional discount); private collector notes; Remove profile (not while they have invoices).
- Invoices list: invoices, newest first, with search (number, collector, work title or artist) and a Status `MultiSelect` (Open and Partial payment by default); numbered pages of `INVOICES_PAGE_SIZE` (25); "+ New invoice" and Invoice settings.
- New invoice: choose the buyer with `PersonPicker` (or create one), then tick works from the whole inventory with `WorkPicker` (any artists; search, show available / on hold / all, numbered pages); works on hold for or sold to someone else, or not for sale, can't be ticked. A side panel lists the chosen works with discount %, sales tax % (default from settings), date and live totals; "Create invoice" puts available works on hold for the buyer and opens the invoice.
- Invoice page: line items (image, caption, editable price, remove, add another of the collector's on-hold works), the printed fields (number, date, "To" block, discount %, sales tax %, shipping, status, terms) and private notes with one Save and version check, live totals, payments (amount, date received, method, note; "Paid in full"; remove), View PDF / Download PDF, Delete (only without payments). Item and payment changes save immediately.
- Invoice settings: gallery name, address, email, phone, number prefix, default sales tax and terms; the env payment details are shown read-only (account number masked).
- Inventory: works, newest first, with search, artist and location filters and a Status `MultiSelect` (Available and On hold by default); numbered pages of `WORKS_PAGE_SIZE` (25) with the same `Pagination` control; filters and page are in the URL.
- Work page: caption fields and inventory number (left blank on a new work, it is assigned on save), images (upload, alt text, drag to reorder, remove), price and currency, status, location and consignment dates, sale (date, price, buyer chosen with `PersonPicker`, notes), private notes, an optional reason for the change, the price/status history timeline, the slideshows that show the work, its invoices (with "Create invoice for <buyer>" once it is saved as on hold for a buyer), Versions and Delete. A new work is an unsaved form until Save.
- Shipping labels (`ShippingLabelDialog.vue`): "Shipping labels" on the Inventory (every work matching the filters, up to `SHIPPING_LABELS_MAX` (200); disabled when none or too many match), "Shipping labels…" in the room editor's settings menu (every work in the room, hidden ones too; disabled while there are unsaved changes or no works) and "Shipping label" on a saved work's page. The dialog takes a free-text Ship to address and a Return address (radio: Gallery address, the default, previewing the invoice-settings name and address; or Other, free text), remembers both for the browser session, and makes the PDF on request, then offers Open PDF and Download (a blob link).
- Duplicates: suggested pairs best first (reasons, match score, both people with contact details, profiles, date added and "from a viewing room" / "from an inquiry"), "Not the same" and "Compare & merge…". `MergeDialog` (also from the person page and visitor dashboard): pick the other person with `PersonPicker` when not given, choose the record to keep (an artist is always kept), pick per differing field whose value to keep, see what moves, then merge and open the kept person.
- Inquiry alert (`inquiries/InquiryAlerts.vue`): above every back-office page while there are new inquiries: up to three (person, item, note excerpt, how long ago, Open → the inquiry on their collector tab, Dismiss), "All inquiries", "Dismiss all" and "+N more". Dismissing marks it seen for every admin.
- Inquiries list: every inquiry, newest first, with search (person, item, note, email) and All / New; rows as on the collector panel with the person; Dismiss and Delete (spam).
- Viewing rooms list: title (with subtitle) and cover (the PDF cover image with its crop at the page's shape, else the first work's), status (Draft / Live / Closed, or Scheduled / Expired outside the window), works, visitors, last visit, Copy link, Duplicate, and Edit or Dashboard (the room opens on its dashboard once it is live or closed, in the editor while a draft); search, a Status dropdown of checkboxes (`admin/ui/MultiSelect.vue`, the shared multi-choice dropdown: the trigger sums up the choice, the menu stays open while ticking, at least one stays ticked; Draft and Live by default, so closed rooms are hidden until Closed is ticked; kept in `?status=` as a comma list), numbered pages.
- Room editor: the link (copy, Preview ↗, "New link…" with a confirm); PDF (cover image via `PageImageField`: a preview at the page's shape (the first work's image while none is chosen), Choose… / Change… and Remove; Generate / Regenerate PDF, disabled while there are unsaved changes; Download PDF and "Remove…" (with a confirm) once one exists; an "Offer to visitors" switch saved with the room; status: not generated yet, generated when and how big, hidden from visitors, or out of date since the room changed); details (title, optional subtitle, status, open/close times) and display (Show prices, Hide unavailable works, introduction in `RichEditor`); works from `WorkPicker` in a dialog as drag-to-reorder rows with status, what viewers will see, a price setting (room setting / show / hide; off for unavailable works), "Hide" (hides the work from viewers; highlighted for unavailable works) and Remove; artists (after the first save), one row each with "Show viewers:" Bio / CV / Press checkboxes (disabled with "none yet" when the profile has none; press shows how many of how many), order, a press checklist, the PDF bio page background when the bio is shown (a colour picker, white by default, or a full-bleed photo via `PageImageField`, which hides the colour) and links to fill a missing bio or CV; work rows wrap their controls under the work on phones. One Save (Ctrl/Cmd+S) sends the details (version check), works and artist settings; a settings cog to the right of Save (`admin/ui/Menu.vue`, the shared dropdown of actions) with Duplicate (disabled while there are unsaved changes; opens the new draft copy) and History; Delete only while no one has visited.
- Page photos (`rooms/PageImageField.vue`, `ImagePickerDialog.vue`, `ImageCropper.vue`, `CroppedImage.vue`, `crop.ts`): `PageImageField` (preview, Choose… / Change…, Remove) opens `ImagePickerDialog`: the room's images (every view of the works in the editor, the artists' portraits) and Upload new… on the left, `ImageCropper` on the right: the image with a frame locked to the PDF page's shape (`PDF_PAGE_ASPECT`), dragged to move, resized from its corners, arrow keys to nudge, Reset frame; it opens on the current choice, else the field's `initial` image (the first work for the cover, the artist's first work for a bio). `CroppedImage` shows an image's crop (or the image centred) at a given shape. Crops are fractions of the image (`crop.ts`: centred, move, resize, refit; tested).
- Room dashboard: Visitors, Visits, Time spent and Inquiries tiles; visitors by engagement (inquiries, the email typed and whether it matched an existing person or is new, visits, time, works seen, last visit, engagement dots); works by combined interest with a bar, viewers, time, zooms and inquiries.
- Visitor dashboard: what they typed and how they were matched (unverified), first/last visit, engagement score with dots and label, possible duplicates with "Compare & merge", tiles (visits, time, works seen, inquiries), works by interest (image with a pointer heatmap: gaussian density on a coarse grid in one sequential blue ramp, switchable, with a less → more key; interest bar; looking, pointer, zoom time and count, deepest zoom, opened, clicks, visits seen, last seen), artists read about, visits with user agent, a timeline of events (inquiries with their note), and "Delete their activity in this room…".
- `useUnsavedGuard` (`guard.ts`) warns before leaving pages with unsaved edits (in-app navigation, reload, closing the tab); Ctrl/Cmd+S saves.

- Admin writes (`src/routes/admin/`: `index.ts` mounts the routers and the admin check; exhibitions, events and slides in `exhibitions.ts`; news, press and home in `site.ts`; revisions, recently deleted and restore in `history.ts`; people and addresses in `people.ts`; works and slide links in `inventory.ts`; collectors' invoices in `invoices.ts`; shared helpers in `util.ts`, list paging in `src/lib/paging.ts`): bodies are validated with the zod schemas in `@twelveten/shared` (`admin.ts`). Every update sends the `updatedAt` it last saw; a mismatch (compared at millisecond precision) returns 409 with the current copy. Each create/update/delete writes a `content_revisions` snapshot and then asks the `Publisher` to re-render the affected pages (the item's page, its list page, and `/` when the home splash links to it). People, profiles, addresses, works and slide links change nothing public and are not re-rendered; their data (prices, sales, contact details, notes) only appears in `/api/admin/*` responses. Postgres constraint errors map to 400/409; malformed ids are 404. Collector profiles, addresses and invoices have no revisions (invoice effects on works are in `work_changes`).
- Viewing rooms (`src/routes/rooms.ts`, `src/routes/admin/rooms.ts`, `src/rooms/`):
  - Links are 32 random bytes in base64url. Public room responses send `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; POSTs with an `Origin` other than `SITE_URL` or `TRUSTED_ORIGINS` are refused.
  - The gate trusts what is typed (`identifyPerson`, `src/people/identify.ts`): the email (trimmed, lower-cased) is matched against `persons.email`, then `person_emails`; otherwise a person is created (note "Created from viewing room …"). Either way the person gets a collector profile and one visitor row per room (`match`: `email`, `alias` or `new`; the latest name and email typed are kept), and the browser is also remembered site-wide (below).
  - The cookie `twelveten_vr` (random, only its sha256 is stored in `viewing_room_cookies`) is `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on https, one year, with `Path=/api/rooms/<token>`, so each room has its own.
  - A visit is a run of activity with gaps under 30 minutes.
  - Tracking batches add to per-work stats (view, hover and zoom time, focus, zooms, max zoom, clicks, inquiries, number of visits that saw it) and per-artist stats (reading time, opens, CV opens, press clicks), append events and pointer samples, and add active time. Durations are clamped to the time since the visit's last activity plus 15 s. Works and artists viewers can't see are ignored.
  - After each change the visitor's `engagement` is recomputed with `engagementScore`; changing which works are visible recomputes the whole room.
  - Viewers see works not `hidden` (and, with `hide_unavailable`, only available ones). Available works carry a price when the work's `show_price` (or else the room's `show_prices`) is on; others carry "Sold", "On hold" or "Not available" and no price.
  - Artists come from the visible works, in the room's artist order: bio and CV rendered to HTML and press (the artist panel's list minus hidden items), each only when switched on and not empty.
  - Public payloads carry no inventory numbers, private notes, buyers, hidden prices or private person fields.
- Visitors and inquiries (`src/lib/visitors.ts`, `src/routes/inquiries.ts`, `src/routes/admin/inquiries.ts`, `src/inquiries/`):
  - The site-wide cookie `twelveten_who` (random; only its sha256 is stored in `visitor_identities`) is `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on https, one year, `Path=/api`. A room gate or an inquiry with name and email sets it.
  - An inquiry stores the person, the source (`slide`, `exhibition`, `room`), links (exhibition, slide, work, room; set to null when those are deleted), a snapshot of the item as shown (title, artist, date, medium, dimensions, image, price, exhibition or room), the page, the cleaned note, what was typed and how it matched (`email`, `alias`, `new`, `known`), and when and by whom it was dismissed.
  - Public POSTs (rooms, inquiries) refuse an `Origin` other than `SITE_URL` or `TRUSTED_ORIGINS`. Gate and inquiry limits are per address in the `rateLimit` table (`room-enter:<ip>`, `inquiry:<ip>`); in development, requests from this machine aren't limited.
- People de-duplication (`src/people/duplicates.ts`, `src/people/merge.ts`):
  - Candidate pairs: the same email or alias (100), the same email name on another domain (35; names under 5 characters and generic ones like `info` excluded), the same name (60) or a similar one (pg_trgm similarity ≥ 0.6, up to 50), the same phone digits (50, at least 7). Scores add up to at most 100; dismissed pairs are left out; pairs with a person who signed up through a viewing room or an inquiry rank first among equal scores.
  - Merging copies the chosen fields (by default the kept person's, or the other's when empty; the sort name follows the chosen name), joins notes, keeps every email either person used as aliases, moves addresses (dropping identical ones; the kept person's primary stays), works held for or bought by them, invoices, inquiries, remembered browsers, the collector profile (notes joined) and viewing room visitors (two visitors of one room are combined: cookies, visits, stats and pointer samples), then deletes the merged person.
  - The merged person's last state is a `merge` revision, which can't be restored.
- Pagination (`src/content/cursor.ts`): keyset cursors. `nextCursor` is an opaque base64url of the last item's sort value and id; the next page is `WHERE (date, id) < cursor ORDER BY date DESC, id DESC`. `limit` defaults to 10 and is capped at 50; a malformed cursor returns 400.
- Collectors: a person whose work is on hold for them or sold to them (`buyer_id` with status `on_hold` or `sold`, set on a work or by an invoice) gets a collector profile automatically; their collection is exactly those works, grouped by artist.
- Invoice status: totals are recomputed on every change. Once an invoice has payments, they set `open` / `partial` / `paid`; without payments the status is whatever was set by hand. When an invoice becomes `paid` its works are recorded as sold to the buyer (sold on the last payment date, sale price = item price less its share of the discount, location `collector` if unset) with a `work_changes` note. `cancelled` (the buyer declined to pay) is only set or cleared by hand and payments don't change it; cancelling releases its works that are on hold for or sold to the buyer (back to `available`, buyer and sale cleared) unless another of their unpaid invoices lists them, so they leave the collection; reopening holds them for the buyer again if still available. When an invoice stops being `paid` (a payment removed, or the status changed by hand) its works sold to the buyer go back `on_hold` for them, sale date and price cleared. Deleting an unpaid invoice releases its works like cancelling. Paid invoices can't be cancelled or deleted and their items can't change; works can't be added to a cancelled invoice. Dates default to today in the gallery's timezone.
- PDFs (`src/lib/pdf.ts`): one pdfmake instance; URL access is off and local file access is limited to pdfkit's standard fonts; the gallery fonts (Germania One, Gentium Book Plus; OFL, in `assets/fonts/`, copied to `dist/fonts/` by `build.mjs`) are read once into pdfmake's in-memory file system.
- Invoice PDFs (`src/invoices/invoicePdf.ts`): Letter, the layout of the gallery's paper invoices: "INVOICE", date ("May 18th, 2026") and "To" block at left, the gallery name in Germania One with address, email and phone at right; items with cover image, artist, italic title, date, medium, dimensions and price; subtotal, discount, sales tax, shipping, payments ("Paid (5/29/2026)") and Total / Balance due; "Thank you!", the payment details (Zelle, wire routing and account, checks payable to; each left off when unset), the terms, and a footer line. Item images are read with `readMedia` (`src/storage/mediaRead.ts`: the media bucket, `/media/…` URLs only, falling back to `LOCAL_MEDIA_DIR` in development); only JPEG and PNG are embedded.
- Room PDFs (`src/rooms/roomPdf.ts`, `src/rooms/pdfExport.ts`): A4 landscape in Gentium Book Plus and Germania One, in the style of the gallery's fair PDFs. The content is `publicRoom()` (visible works in room order, the room's price settings with status labels for unavailable works, bios the room shows) plus the gallery name, email and phone from the invoice settings. Pages: cover (full-bleed photo with a light scrim: the room's cover image, else the first work's; the artist's name when the works are by one artist, else the room title, sized to fit; the subtitle; gallery name and contact along the bottom; white page with black type when there is no photo); one page per work using its first image, as large as the page allows and never cropped, with no margins: works at least 1.15× wider than tall run the full width from the top edge (or the full height left by the caption strip, centered across) with a one-line centered caption beneath; others run the full height from the left edge (square-ish ones the full width left of a 250 pt caption column, centered vertically), with the gallery name top right and a right-aligned caption block bottom-aligned at the inset (artist, italic title, date / medium / dimensions / edition / price or status); captions are 12 pt with 1.05 leading; text keeps a 36 pt inset; one bio page per artist whose bio the room shows, on that artist's room setting: a full-bleed photo under a darker scrim, a solid colour, or white, with white type on photos and dark colours (by WCAG contrast), the type sized to the bio's length, paragraphs justified, and the text centred vertically by measured height (a one-cell table with `verticalAlignment`); a back page with the gallery name and contact. Images are sized for 250 dpi where they are drawn (`IMAGE_DPI`, `workImagePlacement`; the cover and bio photos for the full page: their chosen crop, taken from whichever copy is read after EXIF orientation, else centred), taken from the `w2400.webp` derivative when it has enough pixels, else `zoom.jpg`, else the original, and re-encoded by sharp as sRGB JPEG (quality 82, never enlarged except the cover crop). PDFs are made only when an admin generates one: it is stored at `exports/rooms/<roomId>/<uuid>.pdf` in the media bucket (outside `media/`, so neither CloudFront nor the bucket policy serves it) with a `viewing_room_pdfs` row (file name `<heading>-<subtitle>.pdf`, size, and a sha256 of the content and `ROOM_PDF_LAYOUT_VERSION`, compared with the room now to flag it out of date). The newest is what admins and visitors download and is kept until replaced; `pdf_hidden` on the room keeps it from visitors only, and "Remove" sets `withdrawn_at` so nobody is offered it until a new one is generated. Each generation removes the room's replaced or withdrawn PDFs older than 30 days. Downloads redirect to a 10-minute signed link in AWS; against a local S3 endpoint the API returns the stored file.
- Shipping label PDFs (`src/shipping/labelPdf.ts`, `src/shipping/labels.ts`): Letter portrait, one page per work: FROM (the typed return address, else the gallery name and address from the invoice settings) at top left and the gallery name in Germania One at top right; a boxed SHIP TO address at 16 pt; the first image fitted to at most 504 × 320 pt and to the room the addresses and caption leave (estimated from their line counts, so a label never spills onto a second page); artist, italic title and date, medium, dimensions and inventory number (never the price); "n of N" when there are several. Works come from `summaryQuery` (by id in the order given, by room in room order, or `filteredWorks`, the inventory list's filters and order). Images go through `src/lib/pdfImages.ts` (shared with room PDFs: `readImage` picks `w2400.webp`, `zoom.jpg` or the original, `toJpeg` re-encodes with sharp, `fittedImage` fits a box at `IMAGE_DPI`, 250), three at a time.
- CV PDFs (`src/people/cvPdf.ts`): pdfmake with the built-in Helvetica; lead paragraphs become headings, lists, bold/italic and http(s)/mailto links are kept, videos and press lists are dropped.
- Unknown `/api/*` paths return JSON 404s; unhandled errors return JSON 500s.
- Configuration (`src/config.ts`, validated with zod from the environment): `DATABASE_URL` (required), `DATABASE_POOL_MAX` (default 5), `DATABASE_SSL` (`true`/`false`), `SITE_URL`, `PORT`, `NODE_ENV`, `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (≥ 32 chars), `AWS_REGION`, `AWS_ENDPOINT_URL` (LocalStack), `MEDIA_BUCKET`, `SITE_BUCKET`, `MEDIA_PUBLIC_PREFIX` (default `/media`), `LOCAL_MEDIA_DIR` (development copy of the site media, for invoice images and derivatives of originals not in the bucket), `MEDIA_DERIVE_INLINE` (`true`: make image derivatives in the API after uploads; development), and the invoice payment details `INVOICE_ZELLE`, `INVOICE_WIRE_ROUTING`, `INVOICE_WIRE_ACCOUNT`, `INVOICE_CHECKS_PAYABLE_TO` (kept out of the database and the repo; set in `.env.local` locally and from SSM in deployment). `src/services.ts` wires auth, storage and the publisher from this configuration for `lambda.ts` and `local.ts`.

## Auth
- Better Auth (`src/auth/auth.ts`) mounted at `/api/auth/*`: email/password with public sign-up disabled, the `admin` plugin (`user.role`), UUIDv7 ids, sessions and rate limits stored in Postgres (sign-in limited to 5 attempts per minute, keyed by the visitor IP from `CloudFront-Viewer-Address`, passed as `x-twelveten-client-ip`; locally the proxy's `X-Forwarded-For`), 7-day sessions refreshed daily, cookies prefixed `twelveten` (`HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` when `SITE_URL` is https), `trustedOrigins` = `SITE_URL`.
- Each request resolves the session into `userId` / `isAdmin`; banned users count as signed out.
- `npm run admin:create -w apps/api -- --email … [--password …] [--name …]` creates an admin or resets an existing user's password and makes them admin (`src/auth/users.ts`); without `--password` a random one is printed.

## File storage
- All media is served under `/media/*` from the media bucket (`media/` key prefix): the original site images and PDFs at `/media/images/…` and `/media/docs/…`, admin uploads at `/media/uploads/…`. Content stores these site-relative URLs.
- Image derivatives (`src/media/derive.ts`, sharp): for every JPEG, PNG or WebP under `media/` (not `media/derived/`), `media/derived/<path>/` holds `w48.webp` (placeholder), `w480` / `w960` / `w1600` / `w2400.webp` (quality 80; only widths no wider than the original, so `srcset` widths are true) and `zoom.jpg` (long edge up to 6000 px, progressive mozjpeg, quality 90, 4:4:4), all rotated from EXIF, converted to sRGB with an sRGB profile, metadata stripped, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`. `zoom.jpg` is written last and marks a finished set. SVG and GIF are served as uploaded. In production the `media` function makes them for each upload; in development the API does (`/api/admin/uploads/complete`).
- `npm run media:derive -w apps/api [-- --dry-run] [--force] [--prefix images/] [--bucket …] [--quiet]` (`src/media/deriveCli.ts`) makes missing derivatives for everything in the media bucket, four at a time; with LocalStack (or `LOCAL_MEDIA_DIR`), originals only in `.media/` are read from there.
- `npm run media:sync -w apps/api [-- --dry-run] [--dir …] [--bucket …]` (`src/media/sync.ts`) uploads `.media/` to `media/<path>` in `MEDIA_BUCKET` with content types and a one-year cache header, skipping objects already present with the same size.
- In development `apps/web/vite/mediaPlugin.js` serves `/media/images` and `/media/docs` from `.media/` (with Range support for video) and the Vite proxy sends `/media/uploads/*` and `/media/derived/*` to the LocalStack media bucket.
- `src/storage/uploads.ts`: presigned S3 `PUT` URLs (15 minutes) into the media bucket under `media/uploads/{yyyy}/{mm}/{uuidv7}-{slug}.{ext}`; allowed types are JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, MP4 and PDF (`UPLOAD_TYPES`). The browser uploads directly to S3; the stored URL is `/media/uploads/…`, served from the media bucket at `/media/*`.

## Database

## Prerendering and meta tags
- `apps/web/index.html` holds an `<!--app-head-->` placeholder. `apps/web/vite/metaPlugin.js` fills it: in dev from `/api/meta?path=` for the requested URL; in a build it writes `dist/_template.html` (placeholder kept), `dist/index.html` (site defaults), `dist/404.html` (not-found title, `noindex`), `dist/_room.html` ("Viewing room — Twelve Ten Gallery", `noindex, nofollow`, `referrer: same-origin`, summary card, no image) and `dist/_admin.html` (`noindex, nofollow`).
- `src/publish/head.ts`: `headFor(db, path, siteUrl)` resolves a path with `matchRoute`, loads published content, and returns `buildMeta` tags + `renderHead` HTML (status 404 for unknown, unpublished or wrong-kind pages). `allPaths`, `sitemapXml` (published pages with `lastmod`) and `robotsTxt` (disallows `/admin`, `/rooms` and `/api/`).
- `src/publish/s3Publisher.ts` implements `Publisher` against the site bucket: `render(paths)` reads `_template.html` from the bucket (cached per process), writes `<path>/index.html` (`text/html`, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=300`), deletes pages that now 404, and rewrites `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`. `rebuildAll()` renders every path and deletes stale `exhibitions/*`, `fairs/*`, `news/*` pages. Before `_template.html` exists in the bucket it logs a warning and writes nothing.
- On the client, `useMeta` keeps the head in sync during in-app navigation.

## Seed
- `seed(db, { force })` (`src/db/seed.ts`) loads the original site content in one transaction (old `/images/…` and `/docs/…` references are rewritten to `/media/…`): exhibitions and fairs (body HTML converted to rich documents with `htmlToRich`), their events and slideshow slides (from `apps/web/src/json/*-slideshow.json`), press items (linked to exhibitions), news posts (HTML sanitized with `sanitizeNewsHtml`), and the home splash.
- `seedArtists` (`src/db/seedArtists.ts`, `seed_state` key `artists-v1`, run by `seed` even after the initial seed): creates a person and artist profile for each name in exhibitions' artist lines (matched on the profile slug), links them to those exhibitions, and turns slides credited to one of them into works (one per artist + title + date, with the slide image) linked from the slide; slides whose title ends in an inventory code are created first so their works get that number. Installation views stay unlinked. Idempotent.
- `seedArtistBios` (`seed_state` key `artist-bios-v1`, also run by `seed`): fills empty artist-profile bios from exhibition bodies' bio sections (`extractBios`: each artist's paragraphs start with their name in bold; the most recent exhibition wins). Bios with text are never overwritten.
- Rows are matched on natural keys (exhibition slug, press URL, news slug, home id). Without `force`, existing rows are kept and the run is skipped entirely once `seed_state` holds `initial`; with `force`, rows are overwritten, an exhibition's events and slides are replaced, and `content_revisions` is cleared.
- `src/lib/sanitize.ts`: `sanitizeNewsHtml` allows `p, br, a, em, i, strong, b, ul, ol, li, img, div, span` with a fixed attribute/class allowlist and `http`/`https`/`mailto` links; `target="_blank"` links get `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

## Deployment (AWS, `apps/api/serverless.yml`, OSS Serverless `osls`)
- Topology:

  ```mermaid
  flowchart LR
    V[Browser] --> CF[CloudFront]
    CF -- "default: prerendered HTML (viewer-request function)" --> SB[(S3 site bucket)]
    CF -- "/assets/*" --> SB
    CF -- "/media/*" --> MB[(S3 media bucket)]
    CF -- "/api/* + X-Origin-Verify" --> FN[api Lambda — function URL]
    FN --> DB[(RDS Postgres 17)]
    FN -- "S3 gateway endpoint" --> SB
    V -- "presigned PUT" --> MB
    EB[EventBridge daily] --> RB[rebuild Lambda] --> SB
    MB -- "Object Created (EventBridge)" --> MF[media Lambda] -- "media/derived/*" --> MB
  ```

- One container image (`apps/api/Dockerfile`, built from the repo root; `build.mjs` bundles `lambda`, `migrate`, `rebuild` and `media` handlers with esbuild (sharp kept external and installed for linux x64 next to the bundle by the Dockerfile) and ships pdfkit's standard-font modules in `dist/standard-fonts` (mapped by `dist/package.json` `imports`); the RDS CA bundle is baked in and `DATABASE_SSL=true`; `seed-data/` is copied to `/var/task/seed-data` (`SEED_DATA_DIR`) for seeding) backs four functions: `api` (function URL, 2048 MB for room PDFs, 29 s, reserved concurrency 10; may read, write and delete `exports/*` in the media bucket), `migrate` (`osls invoke -f migrate`, reserved concurrency 1), `rebuild` (daily at 06:15 UTC, and after each site deploy; reserved concurrency 1), and `media` (below).
- `media` (`media.mediaHandler`, 2048 MB, 300 s, reserved concurrency 2, so bulk uploads queue rather than fan out): the media bucket sends object-created events to EventBridge; a rule for `media/uploads/` keys invokes it to make image derivatives. Backfill: `npx osls invoke -f media -d '{"backfill":true,"prefix":"images/"}'` (after the first `media:sync`). The role may also write `media/derived/*` and list the media bucket.
- Network: a VPC with two private subnets and no NAT gateway; the Lambdas reach S3 through a gateway endpoint and RDS through a security group. Nothing at runtime calls other AWS services.
- Invoice payment details: optional SSM parameters `/twelveten/<stage>/{invoice-zelle, invoice-wire-routing, invoice-wire-account, invoice-checks-payable-to}` become the `INVOICE_*` variables (empty when missing). The Lambda role may read `media/*` in the media bucket for invoice images.
- Secrets: SSM SecureStrings `/twelveten/<stage>/{db-password, better-auth-secret, origin-verify-secret}` (created by `npm run secrets:init -w apps/api -- --stage <stage>`) are resolved at deploy time into Lambda environment variables; RDS reads the password with `{{resolve:ssm-secure:…}}`.
- RDS: `db.t4g.micro`, 20 GB gp3 (autoscaling to 100), encrypted, private, 1-day backups (`dbBackupRetentionDays`, the Free plan's maximum), snapshot on deletion; deletion protection on `prod`.
- Buckets: site (private, CloudFront-only), media (private, versioned, old versions expire after 90 days, CloudFront reads `media/*`, browsers `PUT` presigned uploads; CORS limited to the site origin; retained if the stack is deleted).
- CloudFront: default → site bucket with `cloudfront/viewer-request.js` (legacy redirects, `/path` → `/path/index.html`, `/admin` and `/admin/*` → `/_admin.html`, `/rooms/*` → `/_room.html`: the SPA in non-indexed shells written by the web build, with generic meta and no image, so a shared room link never previews as the home page); `/assets/*` and `/media/*` cached; `/api/*` → function URL with an `X-Origin-Verify` header the API requires on every route, `/api/auth/*` included (`ORIGIN_VERIFY_SECRET`, compared in constant time), all query strings and cookies forwarded, cache key includes the session cookie so admin responses are never shared; S3 403/404 → `/404.html` with status 404. Response headers policies: nosniff, `SAMEORIGIN` framing, referrer policy and HSTS everywhere; `/media/*` also sends `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; …` so uploaded SVGs can't run script.
- Per-stage settings: `apps/api/config.<stage>.yml` (git-ignored; see `config.example.yml`): `siteUrl`, optional `domain` + `certificateArn` (ACM, us-east-1), and optional `redirectDomains` (comma-separated). Every listed name becomes a CloudFront alias; the viewer-request function 301s requests for a `redirectDomains` host to the same path and query on `domain` (`cloudfront/function-code.cjs` injects `CANONICAL_HOST` and `REDIRECT_HOSTS`). Other hosts, such as the `cloudfront.net` address, are served normally.
- Domain:
  - `twelvetengallery.com` is registered at A2 Hosting, and its DNS is the Route 53 zone in stack `twelveten-dns` (`apps/api/dns.yml`).
  - Email is Google Workspace (MX `smtp.google.com`, plus site-verification and DMARC TXT records).
  - The web records change in place between targets. `SiteTarget=legacy` points them at the old A2 server. `SiteTarget=cloudfront` (live since 2026-11-16) makes the apex an A/AAAA alias to prod's distribution and `www` a CNAME to it.
  - The site certificate is ACM `8c90db30-…` in us-east-1, covering `www` and the apex. Its validation CNAMEs stay in the zone for renewal.
  - In prod's GitHub variables, `DOMAIN` is `www.twelvetengallery.com` and `REDIRECT_DOMAINS` is `twelvetengallery.com`.

It then runs release steps 2, 3 (migrations only) and 5. The first deploy runs `osls deploy` a second time with `siteUrl` set to the new CloudFront domain.
- Site media: `.media/` isn't in git, so CI never uploads it; `data:push` uploads it and makes its derivatives (or by hand: `media:sync -- --bucket <MediaBucketName>`, then `media:derive -- --bucket <MediaBucketName> --prefix images/` with `LOCAL_MEDIA_DIR=.media`).
- Production data: `prod` is never seeded. Its data comes once, after the stack is up, from the gallery's local database: `AWS_PROFILE=twelveten npm run data:push -w apps/api -- --stage prod --yes` (`src/deploy/data.ts`).
  - It uploads the site media in `.media/` (skipping files already there) and makes their image derivatives locally from the files on disk. It copies back-office uploads (`media/uploads/*`) from LocalStack; the `media` function makes their derivatives.
  - It exports every table except migration bookkeeping and sign-in state (`account`, `session`, `verification`, `rateLimit`; `src/db/transfer.ts`), gzips the export in memory, and uploads it to the media bucket's `import/` prefix, which CloudFront doesn't serve.
  - It invokes `migrate` with `{"import":{…}}`. In one transaction, with triggers and foreign-key checks paused, that truncates the stage's tables, loads the rows, moves identity sequences past the copied ids, and records `seed_state` `data-import`. The uploaded object and its version are then deleted.
  - Finally it re-renders the pages.
  - A stage that has already been imported refuses another import unless run with `--force`. The source and target must be at the same migration. Users come across without passwords, so create the stage's admin afterwards.
  - Data never passes through git or local files.
- `npm run lambda:local` builds the image and runs it with the Lambda Runtime Interface Emulator behind an HTTP adapter on port 3001 (Function URL event format), connected to the docker-compose services; `API_TARGET=http://localhost:3001 npm run dev:web` runs the site against it.

## Testing
- `npm test`: Vitest across workspaces. `apps/web` tests run in happy-dom with `@vue/test-utils` (`src/**/*.test.js`); `packages/shared` tests run in Node (`src/**/*.test.ts`); `apps/api` tests run in Node against the `twelveten_test` database (override with `TEST_DATABASE_URL`), one file at a time, and need `docker compose up` first.
- `apps/api/src/cloudfront.test.ts` checks the CloudFront function's rewrites (including `/admin*` to `_admin.html` and `/rooms/*` to `_room.html`) and that its redirects match `REDIRECTS`.
- `npx tsc -p packages/shared` / `npx tsc -p apps/api`: type-check (`tsconfig.base.json` holds the common compiler options).
- `npm run test:e2e`: builds the site and runs Playwright against `vite preview` (needs the API on port 3000 with seeded data and the local admin). Projects: `desktop` and `mobile` run the visual tests (each page against the committed baseline in `e2e/__screenshots__/`; refresh intentionally with `npx playwright test --update-snapshots`); `admin-setup` signs in once and saves the session to `e2e/.auth/` (git-ignored); `editing` runs the in-place editing flows with that session (edit and save an exhibition, restore from history, date range picker, create/publish/delete a fair and link the home splash to it, news and press create/edit/delete, infinite loading in batches of 10) and the back office (`backoffice.spec.ts`: sign-in redirect, people filters and row navigation, contact details and addresses, a priced work with image, price history and buyer, CV PDF download, linking a slide to an artist from the edit view, holding a work for a collector and invoicing it (totals, discount, PDF, paid in full → sold), the collection grouped by artist, a cancelled invoice leaving the collection; everything it creates is removed) and viewing rooms (`rooms.spec.ts`: building a room from the inventory with hidden press, the gate and a remembered browser, Inquire's subject and body, keyboard zoom, grid and slideshow, artist bio / CV / press, the collector profile's rooms and engagement, the visitor dashboard timeline, a sold work shown as Sold without Inquire and then hidden, merging a duplicate sign-up; everything it creates is removed) and inquiries (`inquiries.spec.ts`: derivative `srcset` on an exhibition page and the zoom copy in the viewer, a slide inquiry with name/email/note, the exhibition Inquire for a known visitor and "Not you?", the back-office alert, Open, Dismiss, the collector profile and the Inquiries list; the exhibition's settings are restored).
