Justin Beachler (b. 1981, Springfield, Missouri) lives and works in Chicago. His work has recently been shown at Grunts Rare Books (Chicago, IL), Weatherproof (Chicago, IL) and LVL3 in (Chicago, IL), The Nerman Museum of Contemporary Art (Overland Park, KA), Granite City Art and Design District (Granite City, IL), Haw Contemporary(Kansas City, MO) and numerous other spaces. Beachler was a Charlotte Street Foundation studio resident and his work is part of the Nerman Museum of Contemporary Art’s permanent collection. Beachler attended the Kansas City Art Institute from 2001 to 2003 and graduated with a BA in art history from UMKC in 2012.

Laveen Gammie (b. 1997, Scarborough, Canada) lives and works in Chicago, IL. Her recent exhibitions include “Gut (Drink it Dry)” at 24 ATM Lotto (Toronto, Canada), “Always on My Mind” at the Barbershop (Detroit, MI), “Untitled” at Daniel Laskarin’s Sunday Afternoon Series (Victoria, Canada), “The Haunting of a Place That Longs For Your Arrival” at LVL 3 (Chicago, IL), “Substitute Equal Amounts: Part 1” at The Logan Center for the Arts (Chicago, IL), “The Air Will Travel Behind” at Stadium Projects (Guelph, Canada), “001” at WIHSH (Chicago, IL). She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the University of Victoria and a Master of Visual Arts from the University of Chicago.

Tristan Higginbotham (b. 1996, Nashville, Tennessee) lives and works in New York City. Recent exhibitions include “Rustle” at Blue Boy (Savannah GA), “Fouling the Nest” (Cleveland OH), “Strange Figures” at Marvin Gardens (Queens NY), “Doing Time” at South Parade (London, UK), and “Serendipity Trail”, Badwater, (Knoxville TN). She has participated in various residencies, such as the NYC Audubon Governors Island residency, Stoveworks (Chattanooga, TN), Byrdcliffe Artist Colony (Woodstock, NY), and Wave Hill AIR (Bronx, NY). BFA from Watkins College of Art in 2018 and works as a state-licensed wildlife rehabilitator and veterinary assistant.

Nico Ramirez Rosas (b. 2002, El Paso, Texas) lives and works in Chicago, IL. Her work has been featured in LVL 3’s Artists of the Week, and exhibited with TUSK (Chicago, IL), GURE (Chicago, IL), and SULK (Chicago, IL) among others. She received her BFA in painting and drawing from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (2025) and has participated in residence programs at HEIMA (Seyðisfjörður, Iceland) and the late Marfa OPEN (Marfa TX).
