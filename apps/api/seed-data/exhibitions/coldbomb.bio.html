Jason Loebs lives and works in New York. He attended the Whitney Independent Study Program in 2011, received his MFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago in 2007, and a certificate from the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts in 2004. Recent solo exhibitions include Kangaroo Stele, Éclair, Berlin; Stele Trail, Ludlow 38, New York; Private Matters, Maxwell Graham / Essex Street, New York; and TITLE STACK SINK RELEASE, Kunsthalle Fribourg, Fribourg.
