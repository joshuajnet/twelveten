Noelle Africh (b. 1992 Chicago, IL) lives and works in Chicago, IL. They received an MFA in Painting and Drawing from The School of the Art Institute of Chicago and a Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematics from the University of Illinois in Urbana-Champaign. Solo exhibitions include "Signal", Twelve Ten (Chicago, IL), "Superstition", Slow Dance (Chicago, IL) and "Loomer", SHED Projects (Cleveland, OH). They have also participated in many group exhibitions including at Galerie Gisela Clement (Bonn, Germany), RUSCHWOMAN (Chicago, IL), Hyde Park Art Center (Chicago, IL), The Plan (Chicago, IL), The Green Gallery - West (Milwaukee, WI), and Patient Info (Chicago, IL), among others. They have a forthcoming three-person at No31 (Duns, Scotland) in 2026.
